### Offset - Rounded Corners

Fusion already has a built-in Offset command, but it explictly creates sharp corner offsets. **Rounded Corners** works simlar, but rounds the corners when it does the offset.

<Frame>
<img
  src="/images/round_corners/offset_rounded_corners.png"
  alt="Feature tab"
  className="block mx-auto h-[223px] rounded-lg"
/>  
</Frame>

You can input a positive (outward) direction or negative (inward) direction and optionally delete the original curve. For multiple curves, each chained curve is operated on seperately. 

<Note>
    A "group offset" option may be added later.
</Note>
