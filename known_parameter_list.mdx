Parameters that work well for the Autodesk Fusion VBit Inlay Plugin and Vectric VCarve. Parameters vary by bit selection, wood species, wood orientation (face grain vs end grain), and the particular CNC machine. These are proven cut results that have worked for people. If you have something that works, [email me](mailto:corbin@corbinsworkshop.com)!

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="15 Degree Engraving 0.005 Tip Width - Amana 45611-K" defaultOpen>
[Purchase the bit: Amazon](https://amzn.to/4oeJ1Zo)
<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="Feeds and Speeds">
 * Walnut face grain: 12,000 RPM / Feed: 15 IPM / Plunge: 15 IPM
 * Cherry face grain: 14,000 RPM / Feed: 20 IPM / Plunge: 15 IPM
 * Hardwood end grain (or less details): 18,000 RPM / Feed: 54 IPM / Plunge: 18 IPM
 * Stepover: 0.0024 in    
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Parameters for 0.100\&quot; depth inlay">
  * `inlay_depth`: 0.100"
  * `inlay_fit_adjustment`: 0.060" (up to 0.070")
  * `plug_surface_gap`: 0.050"
  * `plug_base_thickness`: 0.250    
  </Accordion>
