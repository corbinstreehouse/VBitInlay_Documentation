What's new in v1.22

## New Sketch Editing Commands

These are useful to modify Sketch vectors to be machinable by a CNC router bit.

In the Design environment, you will see a new Modify->[Round All Corners](/round_all_corners_command) command. When you are actively editing a sketch, the Modify drop down will have two new commands: [Offset - Round Corners](/round_offset_command) and [Round Corners](/round_corners_command).

## Other New Features

**Plug Boundary Offset** - [VBit Inlay / VCarve](/vbit_inlay_feature_tab#plug-boundary-offset) commmand - customize how much larger the plug size is around your plug design.

**Add Profile Cutout (2D)** - [VBit Inlay / VCarve](/vbit_inlay_toolpaths_tab#add-profile-cutout-2d) commmand - adds a profile cutout with your selected bit.
